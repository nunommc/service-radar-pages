The problem

Finding a service is easy.
Actually booking it is not.

For customers

Language barriers, uncertainty, hidden availability and unanswered messages — especially for travellers and newcomers.

For providers

Calls and messages interrupt work; cancellations leave gaps; payments, calendars and customer records live in separate tools.

The real cost: unused capacity, non-billable admin and customers who drop off before confirming.

The opportunity

A simpler experience
for both sides.

Customers find the right provider, understand the offer and book in their language. Providers turn demand into confirmed work — without managing every step manually.

Local residents

Reliable, convenient nearby options.

Travellers

Immediate access to services nearby.

Expats & newcomers

Less friction in an unfamiliar market.

Mobile professionals

More customers, smarter journeys.

From discovery to completed service.

Frontdesk

A multilingual front desk.
Connected to the live calendar.

Web and WhatsApp assistant, with voice as a later option. It answers service and availability questions and acts within configured rules.

Answers

Price, duration, location and policies.

Books

Validated bookings, changes and cancellations.

Escalates

Sensitive or unusual cases to a person.

Stays reliable

No invented availability; transparent AI disclosure.

Example conversation

Customer: “Do you have a massage this evening?”

Frontdesk: “Yes. We offer a 60-minute relaxing massage, a 60-minute sports massage, or a 90-minute deep-tissue massage. Which would you prefer?”

Customer: “How much is the relaxing massage?”

Frontdesk: “It's €55. I have 5:30 or 6:15 pm available. Would you prefer the studio or a home visit?”

System: checks configured price and calendar → customer chooses → shows deposit and policy → confirms after payment.
